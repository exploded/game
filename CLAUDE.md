## Project: game

**Path:** `C:\Projects\go\game`
**Purpose:** The Wealth Lab (called StockGame until 2026-10-30): Rob Goudie's investing community for Consortium Private Wealth, which runs it. A monthly fantasy share game (players get $1M to invest in S&P 500 stocks, plus Australian and overseas companies listed in the US; ASX is out until its licence is sorted), plus Learn (videos, live streams, Ask Rob). In player-facing text the site is "The Wealth Lab" and the game is "the share game". The site is at `https://thewealthlab.au` (from 2026-10-30, before anyone used it). `game.mchugh.au` was retired with no redirect, and there's no `www`. The repo, systemd service and app directory are still `game`.

### Stack
- Go 1.26 / Chi router / htmx 4 / SQLite (modernc.org/sqlite) / sqlc / Go html/template

### Key conventions
- **Money**: All monetary values stored as INTEGER cents. Never use floats for money.
- **Holding cost**: `holdings.cost_basis` is the exact total paid for the shares held (native cents, negative for a short). Returns use it, not `avg_cost`, which is rounded. Update both through `market.Fill` and `market.AvgCost` (`internal/market/holding.go`) in every fill path.
- **Stock splits and dividends**: Twelve Data's daily prices are split-adjusted (its default), not dividend-adjusted, and players get no dividends. `market.CheckSplits` (`internal/market/splits.go`, run by the scheduler each New York weekday from 10:00 UTC) applies splits from the US splits calendar once each (`stock_splits`): shares held before the split's open are scaled, earlier stored prices and open orders rescaled, and a reverse split's part share paid in cash. There's no admin tool for either.
- **Exchange rates**: Stored as INTEGER (rate * 1,000,000) for precision. `market.FetchExchangeRates` (`internal/market/forex.go`) gets AUD/USD from Twelve Data at startup and every 15 minutes, loading a year of daily closes when the table is empty. With no rate stored, trades and order fills in a stock priced in another currency are refused, never converted 1:1. Such trades also pay the game's currency fee (`games.fx_fee`, basis points of the converted value, default 50 = 0.5%, set at `/admin/games/{id}`) on top of `trade_fee`: every fill path gets its fee from `market.TradeFees` (`internal/market/fees.go`), and `ticket.js` repeats the sum for the estimate. `transactions.fee` holds both.
- **SQLite**: WAL mode, foreign_keys on, busy_timeout=5000, SetMaxOpenConns(1). The DSN in `internal/handler/db.go` must use modernc's `_pragma=name(value)` form; the mattn form (`_journal_mode=WAL`) is silently ignored. `TestOpenDBAppliesPragmas` guards this.
- **Migrations** (`internal/handler/db.go`): new columns go in `addedColumns` (and in `sql/schema.sql`); columns taken out of `schema.sql` go in `droppedColumns`; one-off data changes go in `onceMigrations`, recorded in `schema_migrations`.
- **Embedded assets**: `assets.go` embeds `templates/`, `static/` and `sql/schema.sql` into the binary. Template, CSS or JS changes need a rebuild. Link static files with `{{asset "css/app.css"}}`, which adds a content hash so they can be cached for a year.
- **Templates**: layouts/base.html cloned per page. Partials prefixed with `_`. HTMX fragments via isHTMX(r).
- **Monthly games**: one game per calendar month, from midnight on the 1st to midnight after the month's last day in `TIMEZONE`. The month's key (`2026-11`) is in `games.quarter`: the column kept its name from when games were quarterly (until 31 October 2026; the `monthly-games-*` migrations in `db.go` did the switch). `market.EnsureMonthlyGames` (`internal/market/monthly.go`, run by the scheduler every minute) creates this month's game and the next, copying the previous game's settings. Players can't create games; admins change settings at `/admin/games` (`internal/handler/admin_games.go`). Nobody joins this month's game by hand: `/dashboard` (where `/` and sign-in land) redirects to it, and it and `GameContext` join a user who isn't in it yet (`currentGame`, `joinGame` in `games.go`). Other games are only linked from the bottom of the home page: `/games` lists this month, the next game (joined with a button) and past games.
- **Game pages**: every `/games/{id}/...` route sits in one `r.Route` in `main.go` with the `GameContext` middleware, which loads the game bar (`partials/_gamebar.html`: game name, cash, tabs, switcher) and sets `users.last_game_id`. `render()` fills `PageData.GameBar` from the context, so handlers don't. `/games/{id}` is the player's home (portfolio, rank, holdings with today and total moves); non-players get the rules there instead. Rules live at `/games/{id}/rules`. Trading happens in the order ticket on the stock page (`stocks/_ticket.html`, sums in `static/js/ticket.js`). Old URLs from before thewealthlab.au (`/games/{id}/portfolio`, GET `/trade/{sid}`, `/history`, `/questions`) were removed on 31 October 2026, not redirected. The ticket posts through htmx: `orderPlaced` (`trade.go`) redraws it in place with a confirmation and the game bar's cash (`ticket-update`), and `tradeError` answers a refusal with a toast and 204, so a trade adds nothing to the browser's history. Plain form posts (Close position) still get a flash and a redirect. On a phone the ticket is a bottom sheet opened by the Buy/Sell `trade-bar`. The stock page's Back link (`backLink` in `stocks.go`) points where the player came from, and `data-back` in `app.js` uses `history.back()` when that's the previous page. The Stocks tab (`data-stocks-tab`) reopens the list with the chip and search the player left it on. Each holding on the home page has a **Close position** button: a POST with `all=1`, which sells whatever is held, or buys back whatever is short, when it arrives (so a double tap can't trade twice or flip the position), and `from=home`, which returns there. Once a game is over, its home page shows the values from its end: Price is `current_value / quantity`, with no Today, since `RevaluePortfolios` only touches active games. Orders still open when a game ends are cancelled quietly (`CancelOrdersInExpiredGames`, just before `FinishExpiredGames`).
- **Phones first**: most players are on a phone. Under 768px the game tabs are replaced by a bottom tab bar (`game-dock` in `_gamebar.html`). List tables use `class="rtable"`, which turns each row into a card: `.c-main` (stock) and `.c-end` (value) on the first line, other cells wrap below with their `data-label`, `a.stretch` makes the card tappable, and `.hide-sm`/`.show-sm` hide or show things on a phone. Other tables scroll sideways inside themselves. Keep inputs at 16px on phones, or iOS zooms in.
- **Stocks page** (`/games/{id}/stocks`, `internal/handler/screener.go`): chips (`screenFilters`: Rob's Picks by default, Mag 7, Tech, Gainers, Losers, Biggest, Most traded, Aussie, International, Crypto, Players' favourites, All) and a sector menu filter one in-memory list, cached per market for a minute (`screenRows`). Each row has the logo, last price, today's move and a 30-day spark (`sparkPoints`, inline SVG). Search looks through every stock and ignores the chips. An htmx request gets only the `stock-list` fragment, except Back (`HX-History-Restore-Request`), which needs the whole page. Rob's Picks live in `stock_picks`, edited at `/admin/picks` (`admin_picks.go`), which clears the cache. Logos show only from `api.twelvedata.com` (`logoURL`), because old Finnhub URLs break the CSP. Overseas companies listed in the US (ASML, TSMC, Novo Nordisk, Shopify, …) are in `market.OtherUS`, labelled "INTL (US)", with their own International chip. Add one there after checking Twelve Data resolves its plain symbol to the US listing. US Bitcoin plays outside the index (IBIT, MSTR) are in `market.CryptoUS`, labelled "US"; the Crypto chip (`cryptoSymbols` in `symbols.go`) adds IREN, COIN, HOOD and XYZ. Crypto itself isn't offered: it trades around the clock in fractions. Other US companies outside the index (SpaceX, SPCX) are in `market.MoreUS`, also labelled "US".
- **Inviting friends** (`internal/handler/invite.go`): while a game's `referral_bonus_pct` is above 0, its home page has **Invite a friend**. `InviteSend` emails the friend (template `invite`) a single-use link to `/invite/{code}`; nobody with an account can be invited, an address once per game, and each player sends one invite per game (`maxInvitesPerGame`), whether or not the friend signs up. `InviteLanding` (public, changes nothing) stores the code in the `invite` cookie and shows the sign-up form. `finishLogin` calls `acceptInvite` for a new account only: it joins the game and credits the inviter `referralBonus(game)` through `CreditCash`, in that game. If that game has ended, the friend joins this month's game and the bonus is paid there (`currentGame`), so a player can get more than one bonus in a game.
- **Interest on cash**: `market.CreditInterest` (`internal/market/interest.go`, run by the scheduler every minute) pays each player a day's interest on their positive cash at the game's `credit_interest_rate` (basis points a year, 365-day year) just after midnight in `TIMEZONE`, for the day that ended. `interest_payments` has one row per player per day, so nothing is paid twice; missed days aren't made up. It runs before `FinishExpiredGames` so a game's last day is paid. Admins set the rate at `/admin/games/{id}`.
- **After-hours orders**: `market.MarketClosed` says whether a stock's market is closed (only S&P 500 stocks can be, from `market.QuoteStatus()`). Games have `after_hours_trading` (on by default, admins change it at `/admin/games/{id}`). When it's on, a market order placed while the market is closed fills straight away at the last close. When it's off, it's stored in `limit_orders` with `kind = 'market'`, and `ProcessLimitOrders` fills it at the first quote dated today once the market opens. Limit orders always wait: `ProcessLimitOrders` skips every order while its market is closed or before an S&P 500 stock has a quote dated today. `queuesMarketOrder` in `trade.go` holds the rule; handler tests override `marketClosed` and use `noAfterHours`.
- **Market timetable**: `market.NextSession` (`internal/market/calendar.go`) works out NYSE sessions from the holiday and early-close rules. It's only for wording ("It opens in 3 hr 20 min, at 1:30 am AEDT tomorrow", "Closes in 2 hr"; `marketclock.go` in the handlers): whether the market is open still comes from `QuoteStatus`, and when the two disagree the wording leaves the time out. Handler tests set the clock with `setMarketNow`.
- **Confirm prompts**: `data-confirm` (on a submit button or form) and `hx-confirm` open the site's own dialog (`askConfirm` in `app.js`), never the browser's `confirm()`. The yes button takes the asking button's label and is red if that button is `btn-danger`; set `data-confirm-ok` when the label alone is ambiguous (a "Cancel" button gets `data-confirm-ok="Cancel order"`).
- **htmx 4**: use htmx 4 spellings (`hx-disable`, `:inherited`, `htmx:after:request`). The CSRF header is on `<body hx-headers:inherited=...>`. Error responses (4xx/5xx) don't swap (`noSwap` in base.html); `app.js` shows a toast instead.
- **Auth**: email sign-in, no passwords (`internal/handler/login.go`). `/login` emails a link and a 6-digit code (15 minutes, single use); an unknown address creates the account. Sessions in SQLite, 30-day expiry (`internal/auth/`).
- **Learn** (`/learn`, public): Rob's videos, live streams and Ask Rob. `internal/media` syncs YouTube (Data API, `YOUTUBE_API_KEY`) and TikTok (Display API, OAuth tokens in `oauth_tokens`), asks Claude for topics (`ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`, structured output), and sends the live stream emails; the scheduler calls `media.Run` every minute. TikTok LIVE has no API: `checkTikTokLive` (`internal/media/tiktok_live.go`) polls TikTok's own unofficial `api-live/user/room` endpoint every minute around a booked TikTok stream (`liveRoom.status` 2 is live, 4 offline), starts the stream, and ends one it saw start after 5 minutes off air; the last result shows on `/admin/learn/live`. Handlers are in `learn.go`, `questions.go` and `admin_learn.go`; templates in `templates/pages/learn/` share `_parts.html` (video cards, click-to-play player, vote button). Public pages use `OptionalAuth`, so `.User` can be nil: guard with `{{if .User}}`, and use `viewerID(r)`. Admin-ticked topics (`video_topics.source = 'admin'`) survive reclassifying. Pasted links go through `parseVideo` in `video.go`. Claude also names the stocks a video mentions (`video_stocks`, cleared and rebuilt on each classify): they show as chips on the watch page, linking to `/stocks/{sid}` (`StockInCurrentGame`, which opens the stock in this month's game), and the video shows on each stock's page. An admin's `videos.hide_stocks` turns both off.
- **Lobby moderation** (`internal/moderate`): `LobbySend` asks Claude Haiku 5.5 (thinking off, structured output, 5-second timeout) whether a message may be posted. A blocked one gets a toast with the reason and a 204, so the text stays in the box (`data-reset-on-success` skips 204). If the API fails, the message is posted unchecked, so an outage doesn't close the lobby. A blocked message is saved with `lobby_messages.blocked_reason` set (`''` is posted): `ListLobbyMessages`/`CountLobbyMessages` take `show_posted`/`show_blocked`, so players see only posted ones and admins see both, or only blocked with `/lobby?blocked=1` (the admin bar's Lobby link, counted by `CountBlockedLobbyMessages`). Admins resolve each with Unblock, Delete or Block user (`AdminLobbyUnblock`, `AdminLobbyBlockUser` in `lobby.go`; Block user shares `blockUser` in `admin.go` with `/admin/users`). The rules in the prompt match the chat rule in `terms.html`: change both together. Off without `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`; handler tests set a fake with `SetModerator`.
- **Contact form** (`/contact`): signed in only. Until 31 October 2026 it was public, and everything sent through it was spam (SEO pitches and bots), so don't make it public again. Admins read and delete messages at `/admin/messages`.
- **Achievements** (`internal/handler/achievements.go`, seeded on startup): granted through `market.GrantAchievement` (`internal/market/achievements.go`), once per player per game, with an in-app notification. Trade counts, holdings and returns are checked after each trade; Social Player on joining a 3rd game (`joinGame`); Market Watcher on a game's 10th watchlist stock; Recruiter after 3 paid referrals; Champion and Podium Finish by `market.AwardPlaces` when the scheduler finishes a game (only players who traded are placed; ties share a place). Locked avatars unlock with the achievement earned in any game (`HasAchievementInAnyGame`).
- **Admin pages**: the row of links at the top of every `/admin` page is `partials/_adminnav.html`, added by `render()` (`PageData.AdminNav`) for admins on `/admin` paths. Add a new admin section there.
- **Backups**: `internal/backup` uploads `game.db` to `s3://thewealthlab-backups/game-YYYY-MM-DD.db.gz` once a day from 3 am in `TIMEZONE` (`maybeBackup` in the scheduler; `media_state` row `backup_day` holds the last good day). It snapshots with `VACUUM INTO` on a second connection, never the app's single one, and works next to `game.db` because `/tmp` on the server is a RAM disk. Off until the `BACKUP_*` keys are set; `scripts/s3-backup-setup.sh` makes the bucket and the put-only IAM user.

### Content Security Policy
The CSP in `internal/handler/security.go` uses a per-request nonce for scripts. `TestTemplatesAreCSPSafe` enforces the rules:
- Inline `<script>` blocks need `nonce="{{.Nonce}}"`.
- No inline event handlers (`onclick=`, `onchange=`, `onsubmit=`). Use a `data-` attribute handled in `static/js/app.js` (`data-confirm`, `data-autosubmit`, `data-nav-toggle`, `data-reset-on-success`).
- Scripts are self-hosted under `static/js/` with the version in the filename. No CDN script URLs.
- Inline `style="..."` attributes are fine (`'unsafe-inline'` stays in `style-src`).
- External images and fonts must be listed in `img-src` / `font-src`.
- Embedded players are limited to `videoFrameSrc` in `internal/handler/video.go` (YouTube, Vimeo, Bunny Stream). Add a provider there and in `embedURL` together.

A wrong CSP silently breaks pages in production.
