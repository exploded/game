Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

1. Who We Are

The Investor Project is run by Consortium Private Wealth in Horsham, Victoria, Australia (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what personal information The Investor Project collects and what we do with it. You can reach us through the contact page.

2. Information We Collect

When you sign up, we store:

  • Email address — used to sign you in and to send the emails you choose
  • Display name and avatar — shown to other players
  • Your confirmation that you are 18 or older, and when you gave it
  • Email preferences — which emails you want, and when you opted in or out of news from Rob

We also store what you do and post on The Investor Project:

  • Trades, orders, holdings, and portfolio values
  • Messages you post in chat, including any that our automatic check stops from being posted
  • Questions you ask Rob, and the questions you vote for
  • Achievements, watchlist entries, course progress, and live stream reminders
  • Messages you send us through the contact page
  • The email addresses of friends you invite, so that we don’t invite the same person twice

When you use the site, our server logs record your IP address, your browser, and the pages you request. We use these logs to keep the site running and to stop misuse.

We also count how much the site is used: how many people visit each day and each month, how often each video and lesson is played, and how often each course and calculator is opened. These are totals only. They don’t record who did what, so they can’t be linked to you.

3. What Other Players See

Other players can see your display name and avatar, your rank and portfolio value in each game you trade in, each trade you make (in the game’s activity feed), your chat messages, and the questions you ask Rob. Rob may read out a question and the display name of the person who asked it in a video or live stream, which may be public on YouTube or TikTok. Other players can’t see your email address.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the share game and Learn
  • To display your name and activity on leaderboards and in games
  • To send you sign-in emails, which you need to use the site
  • To send you in-app notifications, and emails about your trades, games, questions, and Rob’s live streams, unless you turn them off in Settings
  • To send you news from Rob, only if you opt in
  • To email a friend you invite, once
  • To keep the service secure and enforce our Terms of Service
  • To maintain and improve the service

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

5. Automated Checks

Before a chat message is posted, an AI service checks it against the chat rules in our Terms of Service. If the check finds a problem, the message isn’t posted, and you see the reason. Our team can review blocked messages and post one that was blocked by mistake. If you think one of yours was, contact us. The same AI service sorts questions for Rob into topics.

6. Third-Party Services

  • Akamai (Linode) — hosts our server and database, in Sydney.
  • Amazon Web Services — Amazon Simple Email Service delivers our emails, so Amazon receives your email address and the content of each email we send you. Amazon S3 stores encrypted daily backups of our database, in Sydney, for 90 days.
  • Anthropic — its AI, Claude, checks chat messages and sorts questions for Rob, as described above. Anthropic receives the text of each message or question, but not your name or email address.
  • Twelve Data — provides stock prices and company logos. We send no personal data to Twelve Data, but logos load in your browser from Twelve Data’s servers, which see your IP address.
  • YouTube and Vimeo — Learn’s videos come from Consortium Private Wealth’s YouTube channel, and course lessons play from Vimeo. We don’t read or store anything about YouTube users. Videos play in YouTube’s or Vimeo’s own player, which is covered by that company’s privacy policy and may set its own cookies. Video thumbnails load from YouTube’s servers, which see your IP address.
  • TikTok — Rob also streams live on TikTok. Our server checks TikTok’s public pages to see when he’s live, and sends TikTok no personal data. Links to his streams open on TikTok, whose privacy policy then applies.

7. Information Sent Overseas

Some of these services are based outside Australia. Anthropic is in the United States, and Twelve Data is in Singapore. YouTube (Google) and Vimeo may handle your information in other countries when their players load. Amazon may process emails outside Australia.

8. Cookies & Sessions

We use a session cookie to keep you signed in, and short-lived cookies to finish signing in, to remember an invitation you followed, and to show you messages after you submit a form. These are functional cookies required for the site to work. We do not use analytics, tracking, or advertising cookies. Video players from YouTube, TikTok, and Vimeo may set their own cookies, as described above.

9. Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your name and email address are replaced with anonymous placeholders and you are signed out everywhere. Your trades, scores, chat messages, and questions stay, shown as from “Deleted User”, so that games and answers still make sense. If you want any of them removed, contact us before you delete your account. Messages you sent through the contact page are kept until we delete them.

Sent emails are kept for 90 days, with sign-in links and codes cleared soon after they are sent. Backups are kept for 90 days. Server logs are kept for a limited time and then overwritten.

10. Your Rights

You can:

  • See and change your details — change your display name, avatar, and email settings in Account Settings
  • Export your data — download your account details, games, badges, and email settings from Account Settings. To get a copy of anything else we hold about you, contact us
  • Delete your account — permanently anonymise your account from Account Settings
  • Ask us to correct your information, or ask a privacy question, through the contact page

If you have a complaint about how we have handled your personal information, contact us first. If you aren’t satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

12. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us.
