About The Wealth Lab

What is The Wealth Lab?

The Wealth Lab is a free community from Consortium Private Wealth. It has a fantasy share market game, videos and live streams from Rob Goudie, and a place to ask Rob your questions.

In the share game you compete with other players to build the best portfolio. You start with $1,000,000 in virtual cash and invest in real S&P 500 companies, Australian companies listed in the US and big overseas names such as TSMC and ASML, at real prices — all without risking a cent of real money.

How to Play

  1. Sign in with your email address: we send you a link, so there’s no password to remember
  2. Play this month’s game — a new game starts on the 1st of every month
  3. Invite friends — invite a friend by email and get a cash bonus when they sign up
  4. Browse stocks — search S&P 500 companies, plus Australian and overseas companies listed in the US
  5. Buy & sell — execute trades using real market prices
  6. Track your portfolio — watch your holdings, returns, and rankings
  7. Win! — the player with the highest portfolio value at the end wins

Features

  • Real prices for S&P 500 companies and US-listed Australian and overseas companies
  • Limit orders — set a price target and your order fills automatically
  • Watchlists — track stocks you’re interested in
  • Leaderboards — see how you rank against other players
  • The lobby — discuss strategy with other players
  • Achievements — earn badges for milestones like your first trade or a 25% return
  • Analytics — sector breakdown, trade stats, and portfolio charts
  • Multi-currency — trade in both AUD and USD

Important

The share game is for entertainment and education only. No real money is involved. This is not a financial product and we do not provide investment advice. See our Terms of Service for details.
