5. Prices and Market Data

Trades fill at the price we hold when the order is processed, which can differ from the price on the exchange at that moment. While the US market is closed, a market order for a US-listed stock fills at the last closing price. In a game set to wait for the open, it fills at the first price of the next trading session instead.

7. Corrections and Corporate Actions

If a price, exchange rate, or calculation is wrong, we may correct it, recalculate portfolio values and rankings, and reverse or reprice any trade that used it. This applies to finished games too.

Corporate actions are applied as follows, and some may be applied late or not at all:

  • Splits and consolidations adjust your number of shares and average cost. A part share left over by a consolidation is paid out in virtual cash.
  • Cash dividends are credited to your virtual cash on the ex-dividend date, for the shares you held at that day’s market open, at the amount our data provider reports. If you are short the stock, the dividend is taken from your virtual cash instead. Payment dates, franking credits, withholding tax, and dividend reinvestment are ignored.
  • Suspensions, delistings, takeovers, and removals. If a stock stops trading or is removed from the game, we may keep valuing it at its last available price, convert it to virtual cash at that price or another price we consider fair, or remove it.
