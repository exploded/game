Live streams

← Learn

YouTube streams appear here by themselves once Rob schedules them on YouTube. TikTok, Facebook and Instagram can't tell us, so add those here. Everyone with Live streams email on hears about a stream added at least an hour ahead.

TikTok streams start and end by themselves. The site checks whether Rob is live on TikTok every 5 minutes from 8 am to 7 pm, and every minute from 15 minutes before a booked TikTok stream until 2 hours after. When he goes live, the booked stream starts, or a stream is added if he didn't book one (no email goes out for those). Once he's been off air for 5 checks in a row, it ends. If TikTok refuses a check, the all-day checks stop until tomorrow; checks around booked streams carry on. If checks are failing, use Go live now on the stream's page.
