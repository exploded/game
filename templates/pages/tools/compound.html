How it works

Your first deposit goes in today, and each regular deposit at the end of its day, week, fortnight, month or year. Interest builds up on your balance every day and is added to it monthly or yearly. Once it’s added, it earns interest too: that’s the interest on interest. The rate stays the same the whole time.

Important: Results are estimates in future dollars. They aren’t adjusted for inflation and don’t allow for tax, fees or changes in the interest rate. Real returns go up and down, and aren’t guaranteed.

This calculator is general in nature only and does not constitute personal financial advice. It doesn’t take your objectives, financial situation or needs into account.

The figures you enter stay on your device. They aren’t sent to us or saved.
