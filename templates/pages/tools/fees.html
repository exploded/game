How it works

Your balance earns the return you enter, a little each day, and all of it is reinvested. Each day the ongoing fee (a percentage of your balance) and a share of the yearly dollar fee come out. The fee on contributions comes out of each regular contribution, not the starting amount, which is taken to be after any establishment fee.

Important: Results are estimates in future dollars, before tax and not adjusted for inflation. They leave out buy/sell spreads, performance fees, switching and exit fees, and brokerage. Real returns go up and down and aren’t guaranteed, so use this to compare fees, not to predict a balance.

This calculator is general in nature only and does not constitute personal financial advice. It doesn’t take your objectives, financial situation or needs into account.

The figures you enter stay on your device. They aren’t sent to us or saved.
