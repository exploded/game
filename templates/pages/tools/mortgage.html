About this calculator

What it’s for

It estimates how extra regular repayments on a principal-and-interest home loan could cut the total interest you pay and the time it takes to own your home. It compares your loan with and without the extra amount.

What it leaves out, and what that means

  • Rate changes. The rate stays the same for the whole loan. Real rates change, so your actual interest and payoff date will differ.
  • Fees, offset accounts and redraw. Fees add to the cost; an offset account or redrawing money changes the balance interest is charged on.
  • Your lender’s timing and rounding. Most lenders work out interest daily and charge it monthly. Results can differ by a little from your lender’s figures.
  • Future dollars. Totals are the dollars you’d pay over the years, not adjusted for inflation.
